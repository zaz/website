In 1951 Thøger Bang conjectured that if planks cover a convex body, their relative widths must sum to at least 100%:

To understand what this means see my interactive presentation. Or if you just want to see something beautiful, check out the last slide.

This became known as the Affine Plank Conjecture and has remained unsolved for 75 years. We finally prove it for three planks in a plane.

Three planks

These papers give proofs for triangles and for general convex bodies in the plane. Each has an accompanying Lean formalization.
