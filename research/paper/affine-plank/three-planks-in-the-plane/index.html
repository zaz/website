Abstract

We prove that three planks covering a convex body in the plane have total relative width at least one. The planks may have parallel directions or zero width. The proof reduces a hypothetical counterexample to the convex hull of at most six points. Varying the plank boundaries then forces six supporting inequalities, whose slopes satisfy a planar compatibility condition. A cubic identity in the three widths completes the proof. We also give nonparallel equality examples and describe several consequences for equality.
