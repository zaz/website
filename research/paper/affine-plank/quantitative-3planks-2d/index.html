26 October 2026

We prove the Affine Plank Conjecture for three planks in the plane and its sharp quantitative strengthening, which bounds the area left uncovered. We also prove the quantitative bound for two planks in every dimension. The paper includes all supporting arguments for these results.

Companion material

The extended manuscript adds the complete classification of tight two- and three-plank coverings, finite-body reductions, and further discussion of related results.

The Lean proof contains the main proofs and separate stronger extensions to noncompact convex sets. It also proves a complete geometric classification of quantitative equality for compact positive-area planar bodies, at most three planks, and total relative width strictly between zero and one.
